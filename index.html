Understanding Television News in Spain

The Spanish Media Monitor (SMM) observatory studies how political information is produced and represented in Spanish TV news. Our mission is to provide transparent, data-driven insight into the patterns of media coverage, framing, and tone.

Using state-of-the-art language models and automated content analysis, SMM continuously monitors major national channels to measure attention, sentiment, and bias across political actors. Our interactive tools allow researchers, journalists, and citizens to explore these trends in real time.
