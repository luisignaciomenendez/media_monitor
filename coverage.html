Media Agreement and Coverage Diversity

This section explores the degree of agreement and diversity in Spanish television news programs. We measure how closely different outlets align in their coverage and how varied the topics presented to audiences are. The concept of entropy is used to quantify diversity: higher entropy indicates more heterogeneous coverage across subjects, while lower entropy reflects more uniform, repetitive news focus.
